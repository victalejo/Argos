Argos brings your servers to your Android phone and tablet. Connect over SSH, see your projects and tmux sessions, and jump into any of them with a live terminal. Since tmux keeps running on the server, you can lock your phone, switch networks or carry on from your Mac right where you left off.

PROJECTS, NOT JUST SESSIONS
• The same projects as Argos for Mac and iPhone: they live on your server.
• Open, stop, create, rename and close sessions with a tap.
• Turn loose sessions into projects anchored to their folder.

LIVE TERMINAL
• Full terminal emulator over SSH, with an extra keys row (esc, ctrl, alt, tab, arrows and the tmux prefix) and hardware keyboard support.
• Automatic reconnection when you return to the app.
• The terminal stays connected when you switch apps, always with a visible notification and a "Disconnect all" button (can be turned off in settings).

CLAUDE CODE AGENTS THAT KEEP GOING
• Hand a task to Claude Code on your server and lock your phone: the agent keeps working on the server.
• When you come back you see everything it did; approve or deny its permissions from your phone.
• Alerts when it finishes or needs you (Argos checks your servers periodically; no push notifications, no middleman servers).

FILES
• Browse and search the project and preview code with syntax highlighting, Markdown and images.
• Download any file to your phone.

TABLET
• Servers and projects side by side, and the agent list next to the conversation.

SECURE AND PRIVATE
• Password or Ed25519 key; secrets are stored encrypted with the device's Android Keystore and are never copied to the cloud.
• Server fingerprint verification on every connection.
• Bring your servers over from Argos for Mac by scanning a QR code.
• No accounts, no analytics and no middleman servers: Argos only talks to your servers.

Requires servers with SSH and tmux. Argos is free software (Apache 2.0 license). The app is in Spanish.
